Privacy policy

Updated November 26, 2026

Album Mosaic builds photo mosaics and videos on your iPhone. You can use it without creating an account. The developer does not receive your photos, videos, or projects.

Your photo library

Use the system photo picker to choose individual photos, or grant access to some or all of your library to browse inside the app. You can change this access in iPhone Settings. If an original is in iCloud Photos, Apple may download it to your device when you choose it.

Projects, exports, and sharing

The app keeps imported copies, edits, and finished projects in its local storage. Delete a project from the list of all projects to remove it from the app. Your originals and anything you already exported to Photos remain in your library. You can delete those separately in Photos.

Saving an image or video to Photos requires permission to add it to your library. If you share an export, iOS sends it to the destination you select. Your settings for iCloud Photos and device backups may affect where Apple stores copies.

Purchases and tracking

Apple processes payments and provides the app with the transaction information it needs to unlock or restore Pro. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Album Mosaic does not use advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Contact

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and the information you include to respond to your request. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of support correspondence, email xiangguoxin520@icloud.com.

隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 26 日

Album Mosaic 在你的 iPhone 上制作照片拼像和短片，无需注册账号。开发者不会收到你的照片、短片或作品。

照片访问权限

你可以通过系统选图器指定要使用的照片，也可以授权访问部分或全部图库，在 App 中浏览照片。授权范围随时可以在 iPhone 的「设置」里调整。如果选中的原片在 iCloud 照片里，Apple 可能会先将它下载到设备。

作品、导出与分享

导入的照片副本、编辑内容和作品存放在 App 本地。在「全部作品」里可以删除作品。系统「照片」里的原片和已经导出的成片会保留，可在「照片」中单独删除。

将图片或短片存入「照片」时，App 会请求添加照片的权限。分享成片时，iOS 会将文件发往你选择的目的地。Apple 保存照片或备份副本的位置取决于你的 iCloud 照片和设备备份设置。

购买与跟踪

Apple 负责支付，并向 App 提供解锁或恢复 Pro 所需的交易信息。开发者无法获取你的支付卡资料。Album Mosaic 不投放广告，不使用第三方分析工具或跨 App 跟踪。

联系开发者

如果你主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及你提供的内容，用于回复。咨询隐私问题或申请删除支持往来邮件，可联系 xiangguoxin520@icloud.com。
