Album Mosaic support

Email the developer at xiangguoxin520@icloud.com. For an import or export issue, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the message shown on screen. You can describe the issue without sending personal photos.

How do I start a new project?

Choose New mosaic on the home screen. Pick one main photo and 3 to 80 other photos for the tiles. Earlier projects stay under My mosaics. Use View all to find an older one.

What if a photo import pauses?

Open the same project and choose Resume import if it appears. For photos stored in iCloud, keep your iPhone connected to the internet. If the app asks you to select the remaining photos again, choose the ones still pending. The photos already added stay with the project.

How do I save a finished image or video?

Open the finished image or film and choose Save to Photos. If your iPhone previously denied access, allow Album Mosaic to add photos in Settings, then try again. When a film pauses in the background, reopen the project and return to the same film style.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the Apple Account you originally purchased with. Open the Pro purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Apple handles billing and refunds. You can look up a charge or request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

技术支持

联系开发者可发送邮件至 xiangguoxin520@icloud.com。如果导入或导出遇到问题，请附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本及屏幕上的提示。直接描述现象即可，无需发送私人照片。

如何开始新作品？

在首页选择「新建作品」，选一张主照片，再选 3 至 80 张回忆照片作为拼块。以前的作品保存在「我的作品」，点「查看全部」可以找到更早的作品。

照片导入停住了怎么办？

重新打开同一个作品。如果出现「继续导入」，点它接着导入。原片在 iCloud 中时，请保持网络连接。如果界面提示「重选剩余照片」，选择仍待导入的照片即可，已经添加的照片会保留。

怎么把成片存到系统相册？

打开做好的拼像或短片，选择「保存到相册」。如果之前拒绝了权限，可以到 iPhone「设置」里允许 Album Mosaic 添加照片，再试一次。若切到后台后短片制作暂停，重新打开作品，回到刚才的短片样板即可查看状态。

怎么恢复 Pro？

使用当初购买时的 Apple 账户，打开 Pro 购买页，选择「恢复购买」。账单和退款由 Apple 处理，可前往 reportaproblem.apple.com 查询扣款或申请退款。
